FASE 5 — RECONSTRUIR CASH CLEARING 1015
==================================================

Crear función:
rebuildCashClearingByBooking()

Para cada booking current:

cash_in = suma de pagos recibidos con payment_method='cash' y debit account 1015

cash_out = suma de gastos/pagos pagados con payment_method='cash' y credit account 1015

NO incluir:
- zelle
- bank
- card
- AP payments desde banco
- journal entries reversadas
- excluded_from_ledger
- legacy
- cancelled audit-only

cash_clearing_balance = cash_in - cash_out

Si abs(balance) <= 0.01:
- marcar como rounding_difference
- tratar como 0 para cierre

Si balance != 0:
- generar issue:
CASH_CLEARING_UNRESOLVED

cash_out = 300 + 5.99 + 144 = 449.99
balance = 0.00
