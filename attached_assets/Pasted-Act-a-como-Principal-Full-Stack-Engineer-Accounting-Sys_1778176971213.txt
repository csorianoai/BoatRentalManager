2. ESTADOS CONTABLES DEL BOOKING
==================================================

Agregar o calcular estos estados:

accounting_status:
- not_started
- in_progress
- partially_reconciled
- reconciled
- locked

Reglas:
- not_started: booking sin movimientos contables
- in_progress: tiene pagos o gastos parciales
- partially_reconciled: pagos cuadran parcialmente o hay alertas
- reconciled: pagos, revenue, cash y gastos cuadran
- locked: booking reconciled y cerrado contra edición

PASO 2 — Payment Registration
Permitir registrar pagos:

- Zelle
- Cash
- Platform
- Mixed Payment

Campos:
- amount
- date
- payment_method
- account
- notes
- reference_number opcional

Reglas:
- Si payment_method = cash:
  Dr 1015 Cash Clearing
  Cr Deferred Deposits o Accounts Receivable según estado
- Si payment_method = bank/zelle:
  Dr Bank Account
  Cr Deferred Deposits o Accounts Receivable según estado
- Si el servicio aún no se ha completado:
  usar 2500 Deferred Booking Deposits
- Si el servicio ya se completó y falta cobrar:
  usar Accounts Receivable
- No reconocer revenue al cobrar; revenue se reconoce al completar servicio.

PASO 3 — Complete Service / Recognize Revenue

“Mark Service Completed & Recognize Revenue”

PASO 5 — Auto Integrity Check
Crear función:
checkBookingIntegrity(booking_id)

Debe calcular:
- booking_total
- total_payments
- total_revenue_recognized
- total_expenses
- cash_in
- cash_out
- cash_clearing_balance
- net_profit
- outstanding_balance
- overpayment_amount
- discrepancies[]
- suggested_next_action

Validaciones:
- total_payments debe igualar booking_total para marcar paid
- revenue reconocido debe igualar booking_total si booking está completed
- cash_in debe igualar cash_out o quedar explicado
- expenses deben estar ligados al booking
- si hay captain_hours y captain_rate, comparar expected captain pay vs actual captain payments
- detectar overpayment o underpayment

PASO 6 — Manual Adjustment
Agregar sección para asientos manuales controlados.

Endpoint:
POST /api/accounting/manual-entry

Body:
{
  "booking_id": "optional",
  "reference_type": "manual_adjustment",
  "reason": "required",
  "entries": [
    {
      "account_code": "string",
      "type": "debit",
      "amount": number
    },
    {
      "account_code": "string",
      "type": "credit",
      "amount": number
    }
  ]
}

Validaciones:
- total debits = total credits
- reason obligatorio
- booking_id opcional pero recomendado
- guardar created_by y timestamp
- mostrar estos ajustes en el audit trail del booking

PASO 7 — Final Close
Botón:
“Close & Lock Booking”

Solo habilitar si:
- total_payments = booking_total
- revenue reconocido = booking_total
- cash_clearing_balance = 0 o diferencia aprobada
- expenses registrados
- discrepancies resueltas o aprobadas con razón
- accounting_status = reconciled

Al cerrar:
accounting_status = locked
bloquear edición contable salvo usuario admin o manual override auditado.

5. ENDPOINTS A CREAR O VALIDAR
==================================================

Crear o reutilizar:

GET /api/bookings/:id/reconciliation
Devuelve resumen completo del booking ledger.

POST /api/bookings/:id/payments
Registra pago bank/zelle/cash/platform.

POST /api/bookings/:id/complete
Marca servicio completado y reconoce revenue.

POST /api/bookings/:id/expenses
Registra gasto ligado al booking.

POST /api/bookings/:id/reconcile/check
Ejecuta checkBookingIntegrity.

POST /api/bookings/:id/reconcile/resolve-discrepancy
Permite clasificar diferencia:
- tip_authorized
- payment_error
- manual_adjustment
- company_credit
- review_later

POST /api/bookings/:id/reconcile/lock
Cierra y bloquea booking.

POST /api/accounting/manual-entry
Crea asiento manual balanceado.
