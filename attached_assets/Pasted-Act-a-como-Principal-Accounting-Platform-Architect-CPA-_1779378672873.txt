  "expense_date": "YYYY-MM-DD",

  "memo": "string",
  "department": "corporate",
  "attachment_url": "optional"
}

Contabilidad:
Si paid bank:
Dr Expense
Cr 1010 Bank

Si paid cash:
Dr Expense
Cr 1015 Cash Clearing only if legitimate operating cash
Prefer default corporate cash account if available

Si payable:
Dr Expense
Cr 2000/2030 Accounts Payable

Si prepaid:
Dr 1200 Prepaid Expenses
Cr 1010 Bank

Luego amortización:
Dr Expense
Cr 1200 Prepaid Expenses
