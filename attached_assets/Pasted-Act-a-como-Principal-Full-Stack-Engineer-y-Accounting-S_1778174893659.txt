Actúa como Principal Full-Stack Engineer y Accounting Systems Architect para gestion.nadakiexcursions.

Necesito implementar un módulo interactivo llamado Booking Reconciliation Wizard.

Objetivo:
Crear una experiencia guiada para que el personal de contabilidad pueda cuadrar cada booking paso a paso, según el estado actual del booking, pagos, gastos, depósitos, cash, cuentas por cobrar y diferencias detectadas.

Reglas principales:
- No romper endpoints existentes.
- No eliminar funcionalidades actuales.
- Integrar con booking_deposits, booking_receivables, transactions y bookings.
- Permitir asientos manuales controlados.
- Todo cambio debe dejar auditoría.

Crear en la UI un botón dentro de cada booking:
"Cuadrar Booking"

Al hacer clic, abrir wizard con estos pasos:

1. Booking Overview
Mostrar:
- booking_id
- cliente
- fecha
- total
- status
- payment_status
- accounting_status

2. Payment Detection
Permitir registrar pagos:
- Bank
- Zelle
- Cash
- Platform
- Mixed Payment

Reglas:
- Si es cash, usar cuenta 1015 Cash Clearing / Operating Cash.
- Si es depósito antes del servicio, usar cuenta 2500 Deferred Booking Deposits.
- Si es balance pendiente, usar Accounts Receivable.
- No reconocer revenue hasta que el booking esté completed.

3. Expense Registration
Permitir registrar gastos asociados al booking:
- Fuel
- Ice
- Captain
- Cleaning
- Commission
- Other

Cada gasto debe registrar:
- amount
- date
- payment_method
- account_code
- notes
- booking_id

Reglas:
- Si payment_method = cash, acreditar 1015 Cash Clearing.
- Si payment_method = bank o zelle, acreditar banco correspondiente.
- Debitar la cuenta de gasto correspondiente.

4. Auto Reconciliation Check
Crear función:
checkBookingIntegrity(booking_id)

Debe calcular:
- booking_total
- total_payments
- total_revenue_recognized
- total_expenses
- cash_in
- cash_out
- cash_clearing_balance
- profit
- discrepancies

Debe devolver:
- status: reconciled, partially_reconciled, not_reconciled
- alerts[]
- suggested_next_action

Validaciones:
- total_payments debe igualar booking_total
- revenue reconocido debe igualar booking_total si servicio completado
- cash_in debe igualar cash_out o dejar balance explicado
- gastos deben estar asociados al booking
- pagos a capitán deben compararse contra horas × tarifa, si existe esa data

5. Manual Adjustment
Crear pantalla para asiento manual:
- debit account
- credit account
- amount
- reason
- booking_id
- created_by
- timestamp

Validaciones:
- total debits = total credits
- reason obligatorio
- no permitir cerrar booking sin motivo de ajuste

6. Final Close
Permitir cerrar booking solo si:
- total payments = booking total
- revenue correcto
- expenses registrados
- cash clearing balance = 0
- discrepancies resueltas o aprobadas

Estados:
- not_started
- in_progress
- partially_reconciled
- reconciled
- locked

Caso de prueba obligatorio:
Booking Matej:
- total: 700
- date: 2026-04-27
- bank deposit: 250 on 2026-04-23 Wells Fargo
- cash received: 450 on 2026-04-27
- cash expenses:
  - fuel: 300
  - ice: 6
  - captain Pablo cash: 144
- zelle payment to captain Pablo: 111 on 2026-04-30
- captain expected pay: 5 hours × 50 = 250

Expected result:
- revenue: 700
- payments: 700
- expenses: 561
- profit: 139
- captain overpayment alert: 5
- accounting_status: partially_reconciled
- suggested action: classify $5 as tip, error, manual adjustment or company credit.

UI requirements:
- Use step-by-step wizard
- Show progress bar
- Show green/yellow/red accounting status
- Show next recommended action
- Disable final close unless integrity check passes
- Add audit trail for every accounting action

Deliver:
- Backend endpoints
- Frontend wizard
- Integrity checker
- Manual adjustment screen
- Matej test case
- No breaking changes.
